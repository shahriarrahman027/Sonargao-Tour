6 Friends Group Plan Best Days: Friday or Saturday Start: C&B Savar (7:00 AM) Night Dinner: Hanif Biriyani (6–7 PM)

C&B Savar → Sonargaon & Panam Nagar → Puran Dhaka

An end-to-end low-budget one-day heritage & food tour plan. Eat breakfast at home, catch the morning Nilachol bus toward Signboard/Chittagong Road, explore Panam Nagar and Sonargaon Museum on low-cost snacks, and feast on Hanif Biriyani, Beauty Lassi, and Bakarkhani in Old Dhaka before returning by night bus.

Key Rule
Exit at Signboard
Do NOT stay on Nilachol to Chasara town
📅 Avoid Thursday & Sunday Sonargaon Museum closes Thursday (winter hours 10 AM–5 PM); Panam Nagar typically closes Sunday. Friday or Saturday keeps both open.
🚌 Outbound Bus Split Board Nilachol at C&B Savar toward Chittagong Road or Signboard, then transfer east to Mograpara Chowrasta.
🌙 Night Return Cutoff Leave Nazira Bazar by 7:30–8:00 PM for Chankharpul/Gulistan. Direct Savar buses wind down between 10:00–10:30 PM.
